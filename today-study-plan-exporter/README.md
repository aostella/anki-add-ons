# Today's Study Plan Exporter

A simple Anki add-on to export your daily study plan (total number of due and new cards) for selected decks as a text summary. 

It calculates the **total amount of cards for the day**, which means it works perfectly even if you have already finished studying! It will look at your history for today and add the cards you already studied back to the remaining count, giving you an accurate summary of your entire day's workload.

## Features
- **Accurate Daily Totals:** Shows your total study plan for the day, even if the cards have already been studied or reviewed.
- **Merge Subdecks:** A simple toggle to hide subdecks and merge their calculations into the parent deck.
- **Smart Memory:** Automatically remembers which decks you selected and your toggle settings for the next time you use it.

## How to use
1. Open Anki.
2. Go to **Tools -> Export Today's Study Plan**.
3. Select the decks you want to include in your summary.
4. Click **Copy to Clipboard** and paste it wherever you want!
